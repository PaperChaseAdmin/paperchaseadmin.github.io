About PaperChase

Your AI-powered market intelligence platform

PaperChase is an independent market intelligence platform that combines real-time market data, AI-powered sentiment analysis, and automated trading simulation to help you understand where markets are heading.

What We Do

  • Market Sentinel — tracks major indices (S&P 500, NASDAQ, DOW, VIX) and distills market mood from price action, volatility, and news flow.
  • Crypto Pulse — monitors Bitcoin, Ethereum, and the broader crypto market with on-chain sentiment signals.
  • Poly Watch — follows prediction market trends from Polymarket, surfacing the questions traders care about most.
  • Stock Pick — AI-screened stock ideas refreshed daily using fundamental and technical filters.
  • Trading Arena — a paper-trading simulation where AI personalities manage simulated portfolios so you can study different trading styles without risking real money.

How It Works

Our pipelines pull market data every 10 minutes, run sentiment analysis across news and community sources, and update AI-generated outlooks automatically. All data is transparent and timestamped — what you see is what the models see.
